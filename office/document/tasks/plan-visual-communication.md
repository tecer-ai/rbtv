<task-goal>
Produce the plan that lets design implement grouping and form without re-deriving either: what the locked story emphasises, how its beats group into slides, what form each slide takes, and what the design handoff may not break. Also emit, as files, the visual-reference asks this plan needs — and publish the guard value that decides whether the extraction stage runs at all, because a guard with nothing on record leaves that edge blocked forever.
</task-goal>

<scope>
Read surface: the gated narrative lock in the goal's shared `planning/` workspace, and the visual-strategist capability, which is the law for this stage's procedure and output contract.

Explicitly outside the read surface: the brand pack palette, typography, the HTML standards library, and every design artifact. This act is the narrative-to-form seam and it stops short of look.

Write surface: the seat's one declared goal output, plus the emitted brief files under the goal's `planning/briefs/` — parts of that same one product, handed across the shared workspace; appends to the goal's five ledgers; the seat's own working folder.

Runs to completion and checks out BEFORE the art-direction act starts. The two are sequential, never concurrent.

Not in scope: executing a brief, running an extraction tool, adding a gate, ratifying anything with the owner, or specifying any element of look.
</scope>

<done-contract>
1. The declared output exists and its FIRST LINE is exactly `VISUAL-COMMUNICATION-PLAN`.
2. The output carries all six sections the visual-strategist output contract names, in its order: emphasis map, slide grouping, per-slide visual form, form specs, visual-research asks, design handoff constraints. A missing section fails this contract.
3. Every beat id present in the locked narrative appears in the emphasis map with a weight and a one-line why, and appears in at least one slide of the slide grouping.
4. Every slide in the grouping carries a working title, the beat ids it holds, a grouping rationale, and exactly one primary form.
5. Every slide whose form is chart, diagram or table has a matching entry in the form specs section naming its communication type, series or nodes, and units or relation.
6. The output contains no palette value, hex colour, typeface or font name, grid specification, motif, brand token, chart-library name, HTML, or image-generation prompt.
7. Every brief listed in the visual-research asks section resolves to a file that exists and whose FIRST LINE is exactly `RESEARCH-BRIEF`, and each such brief names exactly one wanted input type.
8. A guard value for `visual_refs` is on record for this seat, written by this seat with a mandatory source citation: `yes` when the asks section lists at least one brief, `no` when it lists none. The recorded value and the brief count agree.
9. The gated narrative lock is byte-identical to what it was at the start of this act.
10. Absent a gated lock carrying its first-line marker, nothing was written to the declared output, the refusal is recorded in the goal's `issues.md`, and the seat ended incomplete.
11. No message was sent to the owner and no extraction tool was run.
</done-contract>
