<task-goal>
Produce the interview seed a later locking sitting can build an annotated spine from — a specific audience, the agreed meaning of "locked" for that audience, and the excavated story in the owner's own words — plus one self-contained research brief for every load-bearing claim the excavation left unevidenced.
</task-goal>

<scope>
- **Read:** the run brief under `planning/` (first line `RUN-BRIEF`); the runtime brand pack's voice element; the materials the run brief inventories; the locking capability's procedure and output contract; the machine-writing tells checklist; the owner, live on the goal's own channel.
- **Write:** `planning/interview-seed.md`, and the research briefs it points at, also under `planning/`.
- **Out of scope:** the annotated spine, the frozen thesis and the lock artifact (a later sitting's); fill research of the `content-facts` type (commissioned after the lock); the HTML standards library, the brand pack's palette, the visual ban list, and every other design surface.
</scope>

<done-contract>
Done criteria — all must hold:

- `planning/interview-seed.md` exists and its first line is exactly `INTERVIEW-SEED`. The file is created empty at spawn, so existence proves nothing: a missing or misspelled marker is a non-report and fails this contract.
- The seed carries a named audience section stating role, expertise, what the audience already knows, and what it resists. The literal strings "everyone" and "to inform" do not appear as the answer to either.
- The seed carries the agreed lock definition for this audience, in one statement, written as what must be true for the later gate to close.
- The seed carries the five excavation sections: friction; stakes; claim (or an explicit exploration direction where no claim is yet held); reader transformation; doubt or known vulnerability. Each is present, and none is empty without an explicit `unexcavated` marking.
- The seed carries a `Briefs emitted` section that either points at each emitted brief by path or states, per brief family (deciding spine, audience-intel), that none was owed. This statement is what the downstream research seats are guarded on, so its absence fails the contract.
- Every research brief the seed points at exists, its first line is exactly `RESEARCH-BRIEF`, and it declares its own return keys.
- Every emitted brief is self-contained: an executor holding that brief and nothing else can run it. No brief refers the executor back to the seed, to the materials corpus, or to this session.
- No brief of the `content-facts` fill type is emitted.
- The seed contains no annotated spine, no frozen thesis, no beat ordering, no slide number, and no design language (layout, palette, hex colour, typeface, grid, motif, chart style, component). It contains no markup tag.
- Every external-facing number or claim in the seed is attributed to the owner or to a named material; an unattributed load-bearing claim appears as an emitted brief or as a named gap, never as a statement.
- The seed contains no owner name, account, channel id, host, credential, or absolute machine path.
- Completeness: an absent or markerless run brief is recorded in the goal's `issues.md` and the work continues from the seed; the audience is probed to specificity rather than accepted from the seed verbatim when the seed is vague; a claim the owner states twice in different words is one entry; a claim the materials evidence needs no brief; a run where the interview evidences everything emits zero briefs and says so; an autonomous run marks every derived element `derived` with its source and every unobtainable opinion `unexcavated`, and records both in the goal's `decisions.md` and `doubts.md`.

Outcome map:

- **Complete, owner live** → the seed and its briefs feed the research executors and the locking sitting.
- **Complete, no brief owed** → the seed states so per brief family; the guarded research seats do not run, and the locking sitting proceeds from the seed alone.
- **Nobody reachable** → the excavation ask parks; the seed is derived from the run brief, the materials and any prior artifact, with every derivation sourced and every unobtainable opinion left open and disclosed. The seed still completes.
</done-contract>
