# `<role>` — identity

State the occupant’s job, judgment target, and agent type. A persona helps it choose among plausible actions; it does not replace a checkable result. Name the role for the work it performs, so another workflow can reuse the prompt.

Keep method steps in `<procedure>`, instruments in `<resources>`, grants in `<permissions>`, and the required result in `<io-spec>` or the paired task. A role with two unrelated jobs needs a split.
