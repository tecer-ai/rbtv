# `<constraints>` — judgment-bound rules

Use this section for a rule the occupant must honor across its work and that a command cannot
check directly. Keep it short. A rule tied to one step belongs in `<procedure>`; a grant belongs
in `<permissions>`; a testable result belongs in the task's `<done-contract>`.

Read `ethos.md` when designing the prompt. Refer to a shared rule from its one source rather
than copying a block that future authors must keep synchronized. A prompt with no such bound
omits this section.
