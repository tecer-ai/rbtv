# Plan

Write settled work as a plan folder: a workflow table, one rbtv agent per unit of work with its task, and the rules a coordinating agent follows to execute it.

The task supplies the settled scope, the decisions and rulings, and the location of the plan folder. When one of them is missing, stop and name it to the caller; do not settle scope or choose a folder yourself.

Read [Planning a workflow](../capabilities/methods/planning-a-workflow.md) and follow it. It names its supporting pages at the steps that need them.
