# master-agent

The master is the system-plane agent (KG: `master`, settled-by `decisions.md#d-agent-taxonomy` and `#d-master-seats`): whole-system oversight across goals, the single REQUEST door, and the user's entry point on any cold contact. ONE role over ONE shared state — the goal set, the queue, the store — realized by N live sessions at once, safe because no master session owns private durable state and the store serializes writes.

Four things about it are load-bearing for every other component in this module:

- **ALL user contact routes through the master.** A goal's taskforce, its `leader` included, never talks to the user; the owner-facing 1:1 right is the master's alone (KG: `leader` § Definition; `message` § fields; `decisions.md#d-comm-topology-correction`).
- **The master's act ENDS at the QUEUED JOB.** It classifies the request with the FEEDBACK razor, scaffolds the goal via the goal CLI with the raw ask as its contract, and queues that goal's job 10 minutes out — it does not staff ITSELF in as the scaffolded goal's executor (KG: `master` § Routing fork, which states this clause scoped to the short lane in its own words; `decisions.md#d-master-no-goal-seat`). **The lane fork the clause was scoped to is GONE** — `decisions.md#d-owner-batch1-0808` (3) took the grill, the catalog short lane, and the re-grill bounce off this door, so the no-self-nomination bound now applies UNCONDITIONALLY rather than on one lane (§ De-grilled door, below). **The supersession of `d-master-no-goal-seat` is NARROW and is now ruled exactly** (`decisions.md#d-master-supersession-narrow`, ruling A-25): clause (ii) — act ends at promotion, no self-nomination — **STANDS**; only clause (i), "the master never occupies a goal's seat", is overturned. The boundary is no longer an open question for the apply sitting: self-staffing into a promoted goal's ENTRY seat at promotion is barred; holding the master's own standing run-resident seat is not an occupation taken at promotion and was never what clause (ii) governed.
- **ONE act reaches past promotion, and only at bootstrap.** For a BOOTSTRAPPED goal — one that does not originate from a seat or workflow that materializes goals — the master OPENS MILESTONE 0, PICKS that planning pass's MODE (collapsed or expanded — outside a run the pick is the master's, `#d-mode-pick-leader-or-master`) and RUNS THE MATERIALIZE COMMAND over the plan workflow already present in the goal folder (`decisions.md#d-planning-is-milestone-zero`, `#d-master-scaffolds-at-bootstrap`). Its ground is structural rather than a preference: at the opening of a brand-new goal the taskforce DOES NOT EXIST YET, and someone must create the first seat folders before the team exists. **`d-cos-runs-the-scaffolding` is SUPERSEDED** (2026-08-10): its `chief-of-staff` is a RETIRED role, and there is no later materialize call left for anyone to own — `rbtv-goal materialize` runs once per goal and refuses to regenerate an existing `seats/` tree (without `--force`), and a goal needing one more seat later gets a `scaffold-seats` call from the seat holding that goal's authority (the `leader`). Running that command is NOT doing the planning — the master authors no milestone plan, no task DAG, no seat definition, no cognitive unit. See § Seats for which spawn reason performs it.
- **The master is a SEAT LIKE ANY OTHER — one role that spawns for THREE reasons** (`decisions.md#d-master-supersession-narrow` clarifying frame; `#d-master-in-run-seat`, ruling A-7). One of those spawn reasons puts it inside a live run, holding a seat there. The rule CHANGED: run-2's former `owner-liaison` seat IS the current realization of the master spawned that way, renamed to the seat-id `master`. See § Seats.

## Seats

**The master is a seat like any other — ONE ordinary seat-holding role that SPAWNS FOR THREE DIFFERENT REASONS.** This is the owner's own frame, ruled at `decisions.md#d-master-supersession-narrow` (2026-07-28, ruling A-25) and made explicit here because the earlier authoring did not carry it: what follows is not two canonical seats plus an exotic third variant. It is one role, one mandate, one shared state, spawned in three different places — and the place is the only thing that differs.

| # | Spawn reason — WHERE the master is spawned | Seat-id | Launch home |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A **console inside the tmux session where a run is happening** — the owner is directly in the run and needs the master there | `goal-master` (named by `r-master-seat-homes`, 2026-08-06, at the master seats' ledger; formerly `master`) | Its seat folder at `runs/run-N/seats/goal-master/` in that goal's run (`d-all-seats-in-run-folder`); a full rostered member (`d-meta-seats-are-members`); renewed IN PLACE, never freshly launched, because a fresh launch moves the owner's door |
| 2 | A **console from the rbtv install root on the VPS** — cold, addressed to no goal | `console-master` | The install root — the sanctioned exception to folder-gated seat identity; descriptor and operating artifacts at `.rbtv/goals/_console-master/` (`r-master-seat-homes` — the `master goal` is retired) |
| 3 | A **channel** — the user plainly messages the system's own identity. This is the OWNER'S BRIDGE TO THE SYSTEM WHEN AFK: it talks to the owner through Slack, and being reachable away from the keyboard is what it is FOR (`decisions.md#d-master-harness-agnostic`) | `channel-master` | Its OWN seat-shaped folder at `.rbtv/goals/_channel-master/` — no goal apparatus around it: one seat, many sessions (`r-master-seat-homes`); ONE SITTING PER Slack conversation, spawned by the daemon's `chat-agent` job — a new thread is a new sitting (`r-channel-master-sittings`, superseding `d-master-session-rhythm` for this seat; registry flag filed) |

**What is the same across all three, and it is nearly everything:** the mandate, the request door, the FEEDBACK razor and its two dispositions, the owed-answers act, the owner-facing 1:1 right, the messaging model, and the shared master state. The shared units — `master-role`, `master-constraints`, `master-restrictions` — are the very same unit files all three prompts carry, by reference and never by copy (`PRIN-11`). Each spawn reason gets its own prompt assembly, i/o spec and permissions because its surroundings differ — a different CONTACT SURFACE to read the user on, and a different set of artifacts in front of it (a run has a roster and a log; the `master goal` has neither) — never because its role or its CAPABILITY differs.

**CAPABILITY is the same across all three, and the earlier framing that said otherwise is WRONG** (`decisions.md#d-channel-master-has-hands` as amended by `#d-master-harness-agnostic`, owner, 2026-07-28, correcting this component's own inherited draft claim). The channel spawn is not "messaging-mediated": it spawns **a real session (any supported harness and model)** from a folder and runs commands, reads and writes files, and does everything the two console spawns can. **The CHANNEL is the user-facing CONTACT SURFACE, not the execution environment** — the session READS the contact from it and WRITES its replies to it, and that is the whole of what the channel bounds. **No master seat is tied to a particular harness**: `d-master-harness-agnostic` fixes that master sessions are real sessions with a model AND a harness, both of which stay OPEN BINDINGS here and are never hardcoded into a seat's definition. Every capability-gap statement this component previously drew between the three seats is superseded; where the three genuinely differ, the difference is stated as SPAWN TRIGGER and CONTACT SURFACE, never as hands.

**Observed at the channel spawn, and it is a daemon gap rather than a design property.** The flat `sessions/` spawn home is retired by `r-seats-only-architecture`; the ruled home is now `.rbtv/goals/_channel-master/` (`r-master-seat-homes`, 2026-08-06). Until the bridge/daemon re-home lands, the daemon's interim spawn home is the retired master goal's run-1 — recorded so a reader who observes it does not mistake it for the design. Adjusting the daemon is core-build work, not this component's (the follow-up task is filed on the core-build task file).

**What differs is WHERE, and what WHERE puts in front of you.** Reason 1 spawns inside an ordinary goal's run, so that seat is a rostered member of that run and its work is that run's owner channel. Reasons 2 and 3 spawn addressed to no goal, so both write their operating artifacts into the `master goal` — the ONE system-born standing goal created at provisioning and never via promotion (KG: `channel master`, `console master`, `master goal`).

**Reason 1 is not a promotion-time occupation, and that is the whole point of the narrow supersession.** `d-master-in-run-seat` (ruling A-7) changed the rule so this spawn is legal; `d-master-supersession-narrow` (ruling A-25) fixes exactly how far the change reaches — clause (i) of `d-master-no-goal-seat` ("the master never occupies a goal's seat") is OVERTURNED, and clause (ii) STANDS: on the short lane the master's act still ends at promotion, with no self-nomination into a promoted goal's entry seat. The two never collided: clause (ii) governs what the master does with a goal it just promoted; reason 1 is the master spawning where a run already is, because the owner is sitting there. Run-2's former `owner-liaison` seat is this spawn's current realization, renamed to the seat-id `master`.

**Which spawn reason performs the bootstrap materialization — ANY of them CAN; the OCCASION is what places the duty.** `d-channel-master-has-hands` settles the capability half outright: all three spawn reasons hold hands, so no seat is barred from running the materialize command for want of a way to execute it, and the earlier "2 or 3, never 1 — the channel seat has no hands" reasoning is DISSOLVED. What remains is not a capability bound but an OCCASION: the bootstrap act (open milestone 0, pick the planning mode, run the materialize command — `d-master-scaffolds-at-bootstrap`, `d-planning-is-milestone-zero`, `d-mode-pick-leader-or-master`) happens at the promotion of a brand-new goal, when the run does not exist yet, and reason 1 is by definition a spawn INSIDE a run that already exists. The duty therefore lives in `master-intake` §3a — the procedure the cold-contact spawns (2 and 3) carry — and in `master-traffic-scope`, their task's scope; it is absent from `run-owner-channel-procedure` and `run-owner-channel-scope` because that seat never faces the occasion, never because it could not act. What is under-specified about its mechanics is Open design point 11.

**FORMER NAME.** Spawn reason 1's seat was previously the seat-id `owner-liaison`, and run-2's live folder still carried that name at authoring time. The rename to `master` is ruled (`d-master-in-run-seat`) and authorized on the live run (`d-live-rename-authorized` — the owner closed the pane and accepted the coord.py roster-identity risk). Renaming the LIVE `runs/run-2/seats/owner-liaison/` folder is NOT this component's act and is not performed here; this file renames the DEFINITION only. `owner liaison` was never a system term — `sd-graph show "owner liaison"` resolves to nothing — so nothing of meaning is lost with the name (Open design point 3).

## Parts

- `prompts/<id>.md` — three prompt files: `channel-master-prompt`, `console-master-prompt`, `goal-master-prompt` (renamed from `master-prompt` with the seat's minting, `r-master-seat-homes`). **Whole-file layout since the 2026-08-10 migration** (`d-prompt-task-files`): frontmatter IS the card, the body carries one kind-named XML section per unit in the ruled order, and there is no `prompts.csv` and no `cognitive-units/` tree. ⚠ **The shared `master-role`, `master-constraints`, `master-restrictions` and the `master-mandate` outcome are now COPIED into each file they serve, not referenced** — the format retires unit references outright, so PRIN-11's one-home-per-fact holds at the PROMPT level and no longer at the unit level. Editing a shared section means editing every prompt file that carries it; the § Shared sections table below names which.
- `prompts/<id>.md` frontmatter `exposes:` — `channel-master-prompt` declares `skill: [web/capture/capture, web/browse/browse]`, so materialize mints a `capture` and a `browse` skill loader beside that seat's `seat.md` (`d-seat-exposes-frontmatter`). The other two carry no `exposes:`: the console seat launches from the install root with no seat folder to materialize into, and nothing has asked the run-resident seat for an instrument.
- **Which `master-*` unit is shared and which is seat-specific** — with the run-resident seat named `goal-master`, the prefix collision is gone: `master-role`, `master-constraints`, `master-restrictions` are SHARED by all three prompts; `goal-master-io` and `goal-master-permissions` (renamed from `master-io` / `master-permissions`) belong to the `goal-master` seat ALONE, named after its seat-id exactly as `channel-master-io` and `console-master-permissions` are named after theirs.
- `tasks/<id>.md` — two task files, same whole-file layout: `serve-master-traffic` (the two cold-contact seats) and `serve-run-owner-channel` (the run-resident seat, whose scope and done contract genuinely differ).
- `seats.csv` — mints the three seats; one row mints one seat (KG: `seat catalog`). It SURVIVES the layout migration by design — the seat row is what pairs a prompt file with a task file. **Since 2026-08-10 it also carries the SEAT CAGE** (owner ruling): `cage-grants` names which sandbox grants the seat wants and `rw-paths` its read-write list. Those are not documentation — `ignite/server/spawn/spawn.js` composes the bwrap sandbox by reading exactly those keys back out of the emitted `seat.md` at spawn time, and **absence is the mechanism**: an undeclared grant is a mount never made. Before the ruling they had no source at all — typed into the live descriptors by hand — so nothing could re-render a `seat.md` without silently deleting the seat's whole sandbox. `channel-master` declares all seven grants plus four rw-paths; the other two seats declare none, correctly (the console seat has no seat folder, and nothing has caged the run-resident seat).
- `references/slack-message-format.md` — the owner-facing message-shape reference, gathered by the `<resources>` section of `channel-master-prompt` AND exposed as a `skill` (row below) so any interactive seat of any component can carry it. It moved out of the deleted `prompts/cognitive-units/references/` to the component-root `references/` pool the anatomy guide places it in; it is an INVOKED unit, so it keeps its own in-place file rather than folding into a prompt. Since 2026-08-11 it also carries the owner's Slack-etiquette standard (`1-projects/ignite-planning-hardening/build/decisions.md`, D3/Ask-4-D11): mrkdwn over raw markdown, never paste a file's contents, `❓` on anything needing the owner's answer, `💭` on notes, no thread emoji. Its turn-fence section is SCOPED in place to bridge-read seats, because a seat that sends up the owner channel would otherwise emit the markers as literal text.
- `exposure.csv` — TWO rows. (1) the console seat's, unchanged by the rename (it never referenced the renamed seat); (2) `slack-message-format` as a `skill`, the etiquette standard's one home, which interactive seats list in their prompt frontmatter as `exposes: skill: [master-agent/slack-message-format]` (a sibling-component reference from inside `meta`; `meta/master-agent/slack-message-format` from another module) — materialize then mints the thin loader beside each `seat.md` per CMP-12. The channel and run-resident `master` prompts still have no row of their own and need none: each reaches its occupant through the seat's assembled `seat.md` at materialize (KG: `prompt catalog` § Definition; `seat` § Seat context discovery), not through an install-time exposure method. The console seat is the genuine exception — it launches from the install root, not from a seat folder, so nothing assembles a `seat.md` for it and an install-time exposure method is the only route. A part with no manifest row is not exposed, and that is the correct default (KG: `exposure manifest` § Membership test); `description` is filled on the skill row and blank on the `agents.md` row, per the ruled schema (optional, skill exposure only).
