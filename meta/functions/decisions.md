# functions decisions

Standing decisions about the functions component. Each states the decision and its reason; the skills and the pages of `capabilities/methods/` describe the resulting design.

## One skill for thinking a subject through: thinking-partner

Interview and the six brainstorm modes are method pages under the `thinking-partner` skill, not skills of their own. Reason: they serve one purpose, thinking a subject through with the user, and the user often cannot say whether their subject is already clear; the clear/unclear test is a mode pick inside the skill, where the agent can switch when the subject turns out otherwise, and nested exposure forbids a skill inside a skill.

## Triage stays a skill of its own

Reason: its trigger is a backlog file to rule on, which shares nothing with thinking a subject through; one description would have to cover unrelated work.

## Investigation has no skill here

The former `investignosis` skill is retired; the `coordinate` skill's investigate method is the route for finding out what exists in files or code. Reason: owner decision, 2026-10-08; the ignite pack no longer names it.
