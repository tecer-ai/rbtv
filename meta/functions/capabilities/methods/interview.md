# Interview

The result: the user's thinking on the objective is understood and pressure-tested. Its flaws and the undesired second-order effects of its choices are surfaced, and the captured understanding is stated back to the user.

Inputs: the subject the user already holds, and whatever objective, depth, context and role the conversation already carries. When the conversation has not made a setup item below evident, ask the user for it before the first interview question.

An interview is not a brainstorm. In a brainstorm the two of you build something together. In an interview you find out where the user's thinking stands and help them find its flaws. Ask questions and state challenges only, until the wrap-up: do not co-build, and do not pitch your own solutions. Not building with the user does not soften the criticism.

## 1. Setup

Discover each of these only where the context and the conversation have not already made it evident:

- The objective of the interview.
- The depth the user wants:

  | Depth | Questions | Recommend it to |
  |---|---|---|
  | Relentless | 15–25 | structure, design or understand a product, a problem or a solution |
  | Quick | 5–10 | check a decision for sanity, or work a small scope |

- The context the user wants to share: attention points, documents, areas to focus on.
- Optionally, a role for you to play: designer, investor, client or another.

## 2. Interview

Ask first the questions whose answers may change the other questions you have in mind.

When you detect an inconsistency or a flaw in an answer, challenge it at once, before the next question: every answer built on a flawed premise inherits the flaw. Evaluate the user's choices for undesired second-order effects and name the ones you find. Then resume the questions.

## 3. Wrap-up

- When you reach the question cap of the depth, tell the user, and name the questions you would still ask.
- When you judge the objective reached before the cap, say so.
- In both cases, state the understanding you captured from the interview.
