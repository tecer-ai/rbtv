# coordinate decisions

Standing decisions about coordinate. Each states the decision and its reason; the skill and the pages of `capabilities/` describe the resulting design.

## Investigation has no skill of its own

The former `investignosis` skill is retired; the `coordinate` skill's investigate method is the route for finding out what exists in files or code. Reason: owner decision, 2026-10-08; the ignite pack no longer names it.

## Research and the interview overlap

The coordinator launches workers as soon as each need is known, in parallel with one another and with its own reading and its questions to the user; it reads the main documents itself and gives the bulk of the supporting files to sub-agents. Reason: owner decision, 2026-10-13, after a plan review where the ten readers, the diagram builder and the owner's interview ran at the same time and the findings landed between question rounds.

## The sanity check is a method of coordinate, tiered by model level

Verifying the session's own output with independent sub-agents is the `sanity-check` method under the `coordinate` skill, not a skill of its own, and its two tiers are `cast route` levels: Strong is `--class broad` (L1), Mid is `--class bounded` (L2), priced unless the user asks for quality. Reason: owner decision, 2026-10-15, converting an outside skill a friend wrote. Its trigger is any moment the user is about to act on an output, so it belongs with the other verification shapes (checker, panel) and not with the close; a skill of its own would be a second front door for launching sub-agents beside coordinate, and its original tier rule (the session's model and the next cheaper one the harness offers) chose models outside `cast route`. The claim sheet, the blind angle, the PLACES list for status claims, the level picked by consequence and the reconcile rules were kept as written.
