# `<restrictions>` — prohibited acts

State acts the occupant must never perform while doing this job. Phrase each ban so a reviewer can recognize a violation. Check that the launch environment enforces any restriction that needs a technical guard; prose alone is not a sandbox.

A positive instruction belongs in `<procedure>`; a permission belongs in `<permissions>`; a quality criterion belongs in the task’s `<done-contract>`.
