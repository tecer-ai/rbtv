# Reference — one subject

A reference gives a reader one bounded subject at the moment of use. Check `component-anatomy.md` before creating it. Its frontmatter says when to open it; its body carries the rule or explanation the entry point cannot hold concisely.

Do not repeat a CLI’s `-h`, another guide, or measured runtime state. Link the one existing source instead. Split a reference only when its subjects can be understood independently.
