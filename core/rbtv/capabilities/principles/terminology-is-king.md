# Terminology is king

**Statement.** One term for one meaning, defined in one place, even over the convenience of a new or more familiar word.

**Rationale.** Agents act on the words they read. When one thing has two names, or one name has two meanings, an agent treats one thing as two or merges two things into one, and nothing warns it. A term with two meanings is cognitive load, and the model resolves it by guessing, which is hallucination ([context window](../glossary/context-window.md)).

**Implications**

- Before naming a concept, folder, file, or field, check the glossary and reuse an existing term in its glossary meaning.
- Give a new rbtv concept its glossary entry before any other file uses its name. Define a component's own subject words once, in that component.
- Use a glossary term only in its glossary meaning. For anything else, use plain words that carry no special meaning.
- Use an outside or older name only as an alias mapped to the current term where two systems meet, such as a harness translation. Never use an alias as a folder, file, or field name.
- When a term is renamed, update every current file that uses it in the same change. Leave comparison entries in `decisions.md` as written.
