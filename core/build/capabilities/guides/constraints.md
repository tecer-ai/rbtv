# Building constraints

[Constraints](../glossary/constraints.md) are standing limits on conduct, honored by the model's judgment, not enforced by software.

## Purpose

They bound conduct on every task of this agent, where honoring the limit takes judgment; without them, that conduct has no standing limit. If nothing fails without the limit, do not write it ([Keep it simple](../principles/kiss.md)); where a one-step or tool-checkable limit belongs instead is in [Choosing what to build](choosing-what-to-build.md).

## What good looks like

- Each limit applies on every task of this agent. A sometimes-limit is absent ([Progressive disclosure](../principles/progressive-disclosure.md)).
- No limit is also stated as a procedure step, and [scope](scope.md) does not repeat it ([Single source of truth](../principles/single-source-of-truth.md)).
- No limit is one a tool can enforce ([Deterministic first](../principles/deterministic-first.md)).
- Each limit names the bounded behavior, the reason, and what to do instead. A ban with no alternative fails.
- Two limits on the same behavior are one limit ([Keep it simple](../principles/kiss.md)).
- The constraints hold limits only. They hold no steps and no task files ([Micro agency](../principles/micro-agency.md)).

## Making it good

For each limit, name the behavior bounded, why the limit exists, and what to do instead.

Do not set an order; order belongs in the [procedure](procedure.md). Move a limit a [tool](../glossary/tool.md) can check there, naming that tool.
