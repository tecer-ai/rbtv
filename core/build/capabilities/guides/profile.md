# Building a profile

A [profile](../glossary/profile.md) is the facts about the owner that should shape nearly every answer.

## Purpose

It keeps those facts in the window on every turn, so an agent does not rediscover them. Without it, a standing constraint is missing or buried in a note the turn did not open. Which kind of unit to build is in [Choosing what to build](<../_under-evaluation/guides/procedure documents/choosing-what-to-build.md>). Do not add a second always-loaded biography.

## What good looks like

- A fact here would change nearly every turn. A topical preference is not here ([Progressive disclosure](../principles/progressive-disclosure.md)).
- The same fact is not also in knowledge ([Single source of truth](../principles/single-source-of-truth.md)).
- No health section. A health-driven constraint appears as the constraint only.
- One fact per bullet, dated. A correction replaces its bullet. `until <date>` only when the owner gave an end.
- At most 4,000 characters. Over cap is refused, never truncated.
- The dreamer wrote it. No hand edit.

## Making it good

Use `ignite remember` for a fact about the owner. Do not edit the profile by hand. The dreamer files the inbox line, and merges or moves a fact out when the cap would break.

## Traps

- Copying a vault note into the profile. The profile holds the thin fact and a pointer.
