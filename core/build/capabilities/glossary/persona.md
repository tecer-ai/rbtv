# Persona

A cognitive unit within an agent's [role](role.md) in its [prompt](prompt.md): one or two sentences stating who the agent is — its standpoint, such as a skeptical reviewer — never a character or voice added for variety. It shapes the choices the [procedure](procedure.md) and the task's [done contract](done-contract.md) leave open: when to stop, how broadly to explore, how to weigh risk, how to break a tie. The persona aims; the done contract judges — it never replaces or restates the done contract.
