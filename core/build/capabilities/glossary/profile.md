# Profile

The facts about the owner that should shape nearly every answer, at `.rbtv/memory/profile.md`. One file. Thin facts and pointers. Ignite injects it into every turn of every agent, including a scheduled wake.

A fact belongs here only if it should shape nearly every turn. A topical preference belongs in [knowledge](knowledge.md), never in both. The profile has no health section. A constraint that comes from health appears as the constraint only; the reason stays in `knowledge/health.md`. Employer and company facts live in the matching [entity](entity.md); Who keeps at most a one-line role pointer.

The [dreamer](dreamer.md) alone writes it. Agents do not edit it. "Remember X" goes to the [inbox](inbox.md). Sections are Who, Working with the owner, and Now. One fact per bullet, with dated provenance. A correction replaces its bullet. `until <date>` is only the last words before the provenance, and only when the owner gave an end. Cap: 4,000 characters. An over-cap write is refused, never truncated.
