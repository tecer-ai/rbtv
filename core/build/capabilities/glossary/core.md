# `core`

The [module](module.md) for rbtv's own operation, installation, configuration, and editing, and for the software that installs, runs, launches, and connects [agents](agent.md) on any harness: the [installer](rbtv-installer.md), Ignite with its Slack connection and configuration, `cast`, and `rbtv spark`. Its components can belong here even when rbtv does not need them to start.

How agents behave, communicate, plan, and coordinate work belongs to [`meta`](meta.md); subject-specific work belongs to its own module.
