# Payment gate

The payment gate is the one place that decides whether an agent's payment may leave the owner's account. It decides by code, outside any model, from the owner's settings, or waits for the owner's own decision in Slack, so nothing an agent can type, edit or post approves a payment. It is bank-neutral: the `pay` port command resolves the recipient through the account's [connector](../../../rbtv/capabilities/glossary/connector.md) (`asaas`), asks the gate, and Ignite's waking service sends only what the gate approved by running that connector's command (`<connector> pay resolve|execute|status`); `asaas pay` calls the gate itself until Sara's tool is switched to `pay`. Each connector's half of the withdrawal validation (where the bank calls, the token's header and variable, how its body reads) is an adapter in its connector, `capabilities/tools/<name>/lib/withdrawal-validation.js`, which Ignite loads by the connector's name from `payments.connectors`.

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| Mode | `off` refuses every request; `automatic` approves a request that fits every limit set and waits for the owner otherwise; `case_by_case` waits for the owner on every request. `off` until the owner saves settings |
| Limits | Optional amounts per day, week (from Monday) and month, in the calendar periods of the configured time zone (`payments.timeZone`, America/Sao_Paulo by default), counting the approved, sent and uncertain payments of the period with their fees. `automatic` with no limit set is refused on save, so automatic always has a ceiling. Each save raises the settings' revision; a settings form submitted after a later save changes nothing |
| Request | One ledger row: agent, conversation, connector, method, recipient (name, key or document, bank), amount and fee in cents, note, state, who decided (`rule` or `owner`) and when, the recipient as the service resolved it through the connector, the connector's operation id (`connector_id`) and status, and the receipt. Ids read `P-0007`. The ledger's columns for the connector keep their first-release names (`provider`, `provider_id`, `provider_status`) until the ledger next changes; nothing a user or an agent sees carries them |
| State | `pending` (waits for the owner, who can approve it once the service has resolved its recipient), `approved` (Ignite is sending it), `sent` (the connector has it), `uncertain` (the send may have paid but its outcome was lost; it counts toward the limits until the connector's status proves it sent or failed), `refused`, `cancelled` (withdrawn by the agent while pending), `failed` (the bank refused or ended it, or it was never sent) |
| PIN | Set by the owner on first use of `/payments`, kept as a salted scrypt hash in `.rbtv/config/ignite/payments-pin.json`, asked on every approval the owner makes and on every settings save. Five wrong PINs in a row lock PIN forms for 15 minutes |
| Withdrawal validation | The bank's call before money leaves: Ignite answers from the ledger alone, approving only the operation that names a request Ignite is sending, sent or is uncertain of (by its request id, or by the operation id Ignite recorded when the bank takes no request id), at its amount and recipient, once. A retry repeating that operation, amount and recipient is approved again; any other call naming the request is refused. Each call is answered within 1 s, from a thread of its own |

The owner acts in Slack only: `/payments` opens a panel (mode, limits and what went out this day, week and month; pending requests with Approve and Refuse; uncertain requests; the last ten sent with receipt links), and each pending request is also posted in its agent's thread with Approve and Refuse. Before a pending request is shown, the waking service resolves its recipient through the connector itself; the owner sees that resolution, and the agent's own description only as a labelled note. A resolution whose key, amount or fee differs from the request refuses it. Approve is offered and accepted only after the resolution succeeded; a request whose resolution failed stays pending with Refuse alone. Approve asks for the PIN; anyone but the owner is refused. An agent asks with `ignite payment request --connector <name>` through `pay`, reads and withdraws its own requests, and is woken with the outcome; no verb approves, refuses or changes settings.

Ignite's [architecture](../tools/ignite/documentation/architecture.md) names the files of each part; the [runbook](../tools/ignite/documentation/runbook.md) has the owner's setup and the repairs; [decisions](../../decisions.md) records why the gate is shaped this way.
