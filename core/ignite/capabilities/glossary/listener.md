# Listener

A listener is the inbound side of a [port](../../../rbtv/capabilities/glossary/port.md) in Ignite: one per port, it receives each [connector](../../../rbtv/capabilities/glossary/connector.md)'s events in the port's one inbound shape and hands them to Ignite's dispatch, which decides whom to wake. The messaging listener is the operation `message.receive`; its inbound event (who, group, thread, text, with optional parts) is the one the [messaging contract](../../../../connectors/messaging/capabilities/methods/messaging-contract.md) states, and every messaging connector's [adapter](../../../rbtv/capabilities/glossary/adapter.md) delivers that shape, whatever the service's own.

Ignite's listener is `listener.js`. Slack keeps Ignite's socket: `daemon.js` connects and `fromSocket` assigns identity from `slack.ownerUserId`, never from a field of the event. WhatsApp pushes (`wacli sync --webhook URL --webhook-secret … --webhook-allow-private`) into a receiver on `127.0.0.1` only; the secret is HMAC-SHA256 of the body in `X-Wacli-Signature`, and a missing or wrong signature is refused before dispatch. Gmail is polled by the Google adapter only when `listeners.google.pollMs` is set; the module starts no timer of its own. Owner identity for mail is `google gmail read --verify-sender`, not the From header.

## What the listener decides

Routing is Ignite's dispatch, never an adapter's. For each event, in this order: admission (only a verified owner; a [subscription](subscription.md) does not admit anyone else), thread affinity (a reply follows its thread's agent before any rule), the most specific matching subscription, a judged subscription (a TypeSafe judgment at entry, configured per subscription), then the port's default agent (a Slack DM and WhatsApp: the configured DM agent; mail: `listeners.google.agent`). Specificity is strict inclusion of constraints: contact and group together beat group alone. Equal or incomparable matches are a tie: no turn, the judge is not called, reason `tie`. A judgment error or timeout starts no turn, reason `judgment-error`, and the event is kept for retry. Every decision is one JSON line in `.rbtv/runtime/ignite/decisions.jsonl`. No model judges anything at exit, and a judged route never grants a permission.

An adapter delivers events and keeps the session with its service; the listener receives, admits and routes; a subscription says who is woken. A schedule wakes an agent by time, not by an event.
