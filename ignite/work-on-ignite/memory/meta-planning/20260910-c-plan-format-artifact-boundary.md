## Motivation
The plan format's sizing rules were advice that did not bite. Entry 20260824-c-plan-sizing-law-measure-before added "measure the ceiling, one work stream per seat, split at the spec's own seams"; entry 20260831-c-plan-format-judge-seats-craft added per-file custody re-derived from authored bodies. On 2026-09-10 a vault-side plan authored under both shipped three seats each bundling a judgment phase with mechanical propagation to several devices ("decide survivors, then apply on the mirror and three cards"; "convert on the mirror, then copy to two cards"; "sync three cards, three toolchains, hours of I/O each, in one body"), plus a custody line drafted per subsystem that serialized two seats whose real edit sets were disjoint. Seventeen of eighteen pending seats queued behind one chain and a READY seat idled one to two hours. The owner's diagnosis, confirmed in interview: authors turn the TASK list into the seat list (one task = one seat, however many roots it spans), read an ORDERING ruling ("source first, then propagate") as a body sequence instead of a DAG edge, and "split at the spec's own seams" pointed at the tasks, which were the wrong seams. The owner also ruled that counting files, operations or targets is NOT an acceptable criterion, because bundling related edits is often cheaper and better; the criterion had to be the hand-off itself.

## Design
A written per-seat `## Phases` block judged by one stranger-sufficiency test replaces "one work stream per seat"; a mandatory plan-review seat on a different model rewrites the scheduling surface and HOLDs body carves for the author.

Two structures replace the advice, ruled by the owner after a three-model review panel (fable-5 adversarial, grok-4.6 second-order, opus-5 falsifiability) with a rebuttal round and an opus-5 synthesis; all three verdicts were ACCEPT WITH CHANGES and the panel's 21 edits landed with four owner calls. (1) In § Sizing and parallelism, "one work stream per seat" is replaced by a written `## Phases` block in every seat body — one row per phase: resource written, hand-off to the next, artifact path OR `same-state:` naming the input that cannot be written down — judged by ONE test: could a stranger holding only a file carrying exactly what the next phase needs do it correctly. Where yes, the seat ends there and the artifact path becomes a DoD clause of the producing seat; where a needed input cannot be written down, the phases stay together and the cell must name that input. Rows that hand each other nothing and write disjoint resources are separate seats launched together (the fan-out case the first draft's "hand-off to the next" wording missed — the panel's strongest convergence). Count-based splits are VOID by rule. An ordering ruling is an edge whose downstream targets are GROUPED BY CONTENDED RESOURCE (shared written file, one verification run, one device/host/slot), never counted (owner call D); where one tool writes source and target atomically the cut follows the tool, and a new seat with no named writer command is VOID. Long mechanical I/O is its own seat only when a stranger could drive it from an artifact, its failures are report-and-stop, and the cut frees something the plan can name; I/O inside per-item judgment stays with the judgment. A strictly serial boundary may carry a greppable `Resume point: <artifact path>` line instead of a seat (owner call B). Custody rows are writes-only with a cited body line, concrete paths, expanded sets and a pasted empty intersection. (2) A new § The review before handover: every plan gets a `plan-review` seat on a model different from the author's (recorded as `authored-by:`/`reviewed-by:` at the top of `judgements/plan-review.md`), which reads the WRITTEN Phases blocks (never reconstructs them), records two ceiling numbers per seat, names the artifact behind every edge, re-derives custody, and MAY REWRITE the scheduling surface only — table, DAG, edges, custody, checkpoint rows, empty seat folders with scalar frontmatter, mechanical old-name repoints across a closed five-surface inventory — while body carves return as HOLD payload (name, `after`, resource, writer command, clause-reallocation table, wall table) the author performs in the same sitting (owner call A, narrowing the owner's first ruling "may rewrite", on the panel's evidence that a hand-performed split left four stale surfaces and that a split must invent the sibling-exclusion wall the parent never had). Skipped only when `seats.md` carries no `after` edge and no custody rule (owner call C, replacing a numeric "one or two seats"). Rejected: stronger prose alone (the recorded failure mode), a numeric split threshold (owner constraint), refuse-only review (loses the scheduling redraw a second mind is measurably better at), and full body rewrite by the reviewer (the couplings a cold carver severs, such as a delete-then-copy order that exists because a device has ~3 GB free).

## How it works
A plan author writes each seat's `## Phases` block before its body and applies the stranger test per hand-off; the self-check's new item 12 requires `grep -L '## Phases' <plan-folder>/seats/*/seat.md` to print nothing. Once self-check items 1–12 pass, the author writes `seats/plan-review/seat.md` from the template in § The review before handover (both `<plan-folder>` and `<rbtv-root>` filled as absolute paths, because the seat's working directory is its own folder), launches it with `cast seat`, and on HOLD carves the bodies to the prescribed shape, after which the reviewer re-runs the mechanical checks and stamps its row; the author records `author approved <date>` in the `plan-review` row after approving four things (table+DAG+rules, the reviewer's file-list diff, the old-name grep, and the walls and DoD of every touched body). Item 13 of the self-check ticks against the verdict file's contents. The `seats/<name>/seat.md` section now lists the Phases block as a mandatory body element and the `seats.md` section admits `plan-review` as the second non-work row.

## Consequences
Replaces the "one work stream per seat / split at the spec's own seams" bullet of the 2026-08-24 entry; extends its custody bullet and the 2026-08-31 entry's per-file custody with the writes-only, cited-line, pasted-intersection form. Adds two tree entries (`seats/<name>/seat.pre-split.md`, `judgements/plan-review.md`), one section, and self-check items 12–13. Every plan now costs one extra seat run before handover, and every seat body grows by one small table. Plans authored before this commit carry no Phases blocks; the review of such a plan FAILs every seat at bar item 1 by design, so a legacy plan is re-authored, never reviewed as-is. The 2026-09-10 plan was re-split by hand in the same session (levers-apply made mirror-only, card-sync split by device) before this entry existed and does not carry the new blocks.

## ATTENTION
- Counting files, operations, targets or steps is explicitly NOT the rule and a SPLIT whose stated reason is a number is VOID by the text — do not reintroduce a numeric split threshold, a "one seat per target" clause, or a seat-count exemption; the owner rejected each by name and the reference now forbids them.
- The test is stranger-SUFFICIENCY, not artifact existence: any state can be written to a file, so "the hand-off CAN be a durable artifact" is near-universally true and splits everything; the wording must stay "a file carrying exactly what the next phase needs".
- A rule that inspects only hand-offs to the NEXT phase is blind to fan-out — sibling phases consuming one upstream artifact hand each other nothing, which is how the worst-scoped seat of 2026-09-10 would have passed the first draft; the "rows that hand each other nothing and write disjoint resources are separate seats" sentence is load-bearing.
- The reviewer may not carve bodies because a boundary change IS a wall change (the sibling-exclusion wall must be invented) and cold carving severs couplings no grep sees; widening it back to full rewrite reopens both defects the panel documented.
- The reviewer template's toolkit path is a `<rbtv-root>` placeholder on purpose: `cast seat` launches with the seat folder as working directory, where a repo-relative `meta/planning/...` path resolves nowhere.
- Count-based splits are VOID by the text: never reintroduce a numeric split threshold, a one-seat-per-target clause, or a seat-count review exemption
