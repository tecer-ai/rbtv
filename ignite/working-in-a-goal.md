## What you may touch — the fence

Your sandbox is a **fence**, not a cage: one plan-time compiler turns the template, the deny
list and the daemon-owned records into the bind list every worker seat of your goal gets. A
path outside it is a refusal at launch, not a silent failure at write time.

## The goal's own planning tree — source vs derived

`<goal>/planning/current/` is **source**: edit it. `<goal>/planning/current/seat-lane/` is
**derived** — regenerated by the lane builder on every materialize, so an edit there is
silently lost. A derived tree root carries a `DERIVED.md` naming its `source:` and its
regenerator, and every kit write door refuses a write under one.

→ `spec-component-map.md` §4 "Derived-tree convention" · the door and its refusal:
[`planning/component.md`](planning/component.md) · [`coord/component.md`](coord/component.md)
